Study Kingdom 0.1.0 beta

Create a username and password and enter your village immediately. Study to earn resources, upgrade through ten HQ tiers, train from the full roster, and play twenty campaign bases. Friends, private replays, leagues, study rooms, clans, donations and direct 5v5 wars are included. Progress is shared between the web and Mac app.

Play: https://study-kingdom.yuvraj-658.workers.dev

Mac: aarch64 is Apple silicon; x86_64 is Intel. macOS 12.3 or later is required. These beta builds are ad-hoc signed and are not Apple-notarized; macOS may require Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. Updater bundles are cryptographically signed.

Forgotten passwords use an owner-assisted reset; signed-in players can change their password in Settings. Email addresses and email confirmation are not required.

Fresh-machine installation/update checks, older Intel hardware performance, Apple notarization and the two-week friend-group beta remain open. See docs/beta.md.
